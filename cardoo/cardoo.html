How Cardoo Works

Cards & Lanes

Drag a card between lanes to move it. Click any card to open its full text and edit it — the lane only ever shows the first line, so longer notes stay out of the way until you need them.

Reordering

Drag a card and hold it between two others for about a second — a gap opens up between them. Drop there and the card lands in that exact spot instead of at the bottom of the lane.

Highlighting

Wrap any text in double angle brackets to highlight it, both in the lane and in the card's full view: <<like this>> becomes like this.

Save vs. Recovery Backup

Save is automatic and instant — every change is written to your browser's storage right away. It survives closing the tab or restarting your computer, but not clearing your browser's data.

Recovery Backup is a separate, optional safety net: a real copy of your board written to a folder on disk, armed in ⚙️ Config. Once armed, it writes automatically every 20 changes or 20 minutes, whichever comes first — so it survives even a cleared cache. You can also back up manually anytime with 📥 Export, and restore any backup with 📤 Import.

Undo & Redo

Card moves can be undone with Ctrl+Z and redone with Ctrl+Y.
